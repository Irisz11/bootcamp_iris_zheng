# Stakeholder Brief — Weekly ETF Rebalancing Risk Support

**Audience:** Portfolio Manager / Investment Analyst  
**Cadence:** Weekly  
**Decision Supported:** Review whether current ETF exposure should be maintained, reduced, or rebalanced.

## Context

The purpose of this project is to support weekly ETF rebalancing decisions by providing a structured view of recent return, volatility, and market risk conditions. Historical strategy performance may not remain reliable when market regimes, liquidity, transaction costs, or other conditions change. The analysis is therefore intended to help the portfolio manager identify when current exposure may require closer review rather than relying only on historical performance.

## What You'll Receive

- Weekly return and volatility summaries
- Forward-looking risk bands or prediction intervals
- Plain-language scenario notes explaining current market conditions
- Clear decision triggers indicating when portfolio exposure should be reviewed

## Assumptions & Constraints

- Historical return and volatility patterns provide some information about near-term risk.
- ETF prices and trading data are sufficiently liquid and reliable for analysis.
- Transaction costs and market impact may affect the usefulness of a rebalance signal.
- Historical relationships may change across market regimes.
- Data must be available and refreshed in time for a weekly decision process.
- The analysis is designed as decision support, not as an automated trading system.

## Key Risks

- Market regime changes may reduce the reliability of historical relationships.
- Transaction costs and liquidity conditions may change over time.
- Forecasting performance may deteriorate outside the historical sample.
- Benchmark and evaluation metric choices may materially affect conclusions.
